# Cycles hold every course type; programmes are derived from them

A cycle is one intake (season, start year, space) and holds every course offered in it, whatever its type. The Bridge Programme is not a container of courses. It links to its two semester cycles, and its courses are those cycles' Core, Thematic and Lab courses. The academic year is derived from when the cycle starts (September boundary), never typed in. The old site published one intake across several pages (short courses, semester courses, labs) with one deadline, and this model keeps it as one intake.

## Considered Options

- **Separate "semester" and "short course" cycles**: this splits one intake and its deadline in two, and needs manual programme binding (the bug in the current seed).
- **Courses attached directly to programmes**: this duplicates the cycle relationship and lets the two disagree.
