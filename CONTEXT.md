# CILAS

The Cairo Institute for Liberal Arts and Sciences: an independent school that runs seasonal cycles of courses, longer degree-like programmes such as the Bridge, and publishes work by its community. This site is its public face, its course catalogue and its archive since 2013.

## Language

### Time and place

**Cycle**:
One intake of courses in a given season, start year and space (e.g. Spring 2026, Cairo). It holds every course type offered in that intake.
_Avoid_: Term, trimester, semester (as entity names)

**Season**:
The part of the year a cycle runs in: fall, winter, spring or summer. Legacy trimesters map onto fall, winter and spring.
_Avoid_: Trimester

**Academic year**:
A September-to-August year (e.g. 2025–2026), derived from when a cycle starts, never entered by hand.

**Space**:
A physical site where CILAS has taught, such as Darb El Labbana in Cairo or Al Manshiya in Alexandria. It is either current or former.
_Avoid_: Venue, campus, branch

### Programmes

**Programme**:
A structured path through several cycles that people join as a cohort, with its own admission, pricing and marketing. Kinds: Bridge, Women's Studies, Residency.
_Avoid_: Track, degree

**Bridge Programme**:
CILAS's year-long liberal-arts programme. It spans two semester cycles of one academic year and is made up of that year's Core and Thematic courses and Labs.
_Avoid_: Foundation year, fellowship programme

**Component**:
A named part of a programme (e.g. "Core courses, fall semester") defined by a cycle and the course types it draws from.

### Teaching

**Course**:
One offering of teaching within one cycle, of exactly one course type.
_Avoid_: Class, module

**Course type**:
The kind of course: Core, Thematic, Lab, Workshop or Short. Core, Thematic and Lab courses count toward the Bridge; Workshop and Short courses do not.

**Schedule**:
The recurring pattern a course meets on: a start date, a number of weeks and weekly slots. Times are Cairo local time.
_Avoid_: Timetable

**Slot**:
One weekly meeting time in a schedule: a weekday with start and end times.
_Avoid_: Time slot, section

**Session**:
One unit of a course's flow, usually a week, with its own topic, materials and activities. It may span several meetings. A course numbers its sessions either as weeks or as sessions, following how its fellow wrote the flow.
_Avoid_: Week, lecture, class

**Meeting**:
One dated occurrence of a course, produced by the schedule's slots. Its date can be moved or cancelled.
_Avoid_: Class, lecture

**Material**:
An item a session or course asks participants to engage with: read, watch, listen, a prompt, or other.
_Avoid_: Reference, resource

**Activity**:
Something participants do during a session rather than engage with beforehand: a discussion, field visit, exercise, screening or presentation.
_Avoid_: Assignment, task

**Course flow**:
The legacy syllabus document (usually a PDF) a fellow wrote for a course. Its contents now live in the course itself; the document is not kept.
_Avoid_: Syllabus file, workflow

### People

**Person**:
Someone who appears on the site: a fellow, a student, an author, an advisor, a speaker. A person need not have an account.
_Avoid_: Profile, member

**User**:
A login account. It may be linked to one person.
_Avoid_: Account holder, member

**Fellow**:
A person who teaches a course.
_Avoid_: Instructor, tutor, teacher, faculty

**CILASian**:
A member of a programme's community for a given year, whether a student or a fellow. Taking a single course does not make someone a CILASian.
_Avoid_: Bridge student, alumnus

**Residential fellow** / **Visiting fellow**:
A fellow who is a CILASian for a year: residential fellows carry the programme (coordinating, directing); visiting fellows come to coordinate one course.

**Non-degree-seeking student**:
A person who takes individual courses without joining a programme.
_Avoid_: External student, auditor

**Membership**:
A person's place in one programme's community, with a role: student, residential fellow or visiting fellow. Every membership makes that person a CILASian of that programme. A person can hold many.

**Enrollment**:
A person's place in one course, with its status.
_Avoid_: Registration (that is the act of applying)

**Advisor** / **Trustee**:
A person on CILAS's board of advisors or board of trustees for a given term.

**Partner**:
An outside organisation CILAS works with: funders, co-hosts, press outlets.

### Taking part

**Registration**:
How a person applies for a course, event or programme: a site form, an external link, or closed.
_Avoid_: Sign-up, enrollment

**Deadline**:
The last day registration is open. A course uses its cycle's deadline unless it sets its own.

**Pricing**:
What something costs, as a range in EGP, with optional payment plans of instalments.
_Avoid_: Tuition, fees

**Need-based deduction**:
A reduced price granted to people who cannot afford the full one.

### Publishing

**Tier**:
Who may see a piece of content: public (everyone), community (signed-in people) or owner (its owners and admins).
_Avoid_: Visibility, access level

**Requested tier**:
The tier an author asks for when submitting an article. Only an admin sets the actual tier.

**Article**:
A piece of writing by members of the community, published after review.
_Avoid_: Post, blog post

**Related article**:
An article shown under another as further reading: those an editor picked, then others from the same publication, then ones sharing a tag, then others by the same author — at most three, and only ones the reader may see.

**Review**:
The approval path an article, course or publication goes through: draft, submitted, changes requested, then approved or rejected. Once it is published, its owners change it or take it down only through review: the site keeps the approved version until an admin approves the change or the removal.

**Publication**:
An edited collection of articles, often produced by a course or lab, with a foreword and credits.
_Avoid_: Article collection, anthology, booklet

**Credit**:
A named contribution to an article or publication (author, translator, editor, illustrator…) by one or more people.

**Press item**:
Coverage of CILAS by an outside outlet.
_Avoid_: Press release, media mention

**Event**:
A one-off or recurring gathering that is not a course: talk, open day, screening.

**Newsletter**:
An issue CILAS sends to subscribers and keeps in its archive. The subscriber list lives with the email service, not on the site.

### Reading the site

**Site language**:
The language a visitor reads the site in: English or Arabic. Every public page exists once per site language, and the language is part of its address.
_Avoid_: Locale (in conversation), translation

**Content language**:
The language a piece of content is written in. When a piece has no text in the site language, it is shown in its content language, marked as such, while the rest of the page stays in the site language.

### Archive

**Legacy URL**:
The address an imported piece of content had on the old Weebly site, kept so old links can redirect.

**Import status**:
Whether imported content has been checked by a person (needs review, reviewed).
